**Notes**
1. Amazon itself isn't selling this one new; the price is the lowest new offer from another seller. For AirPods 4 that's DataVision; for Pro 2 it's 6ave. There's also an older Pro 2 USB-C listing, B0CHWRXH8B (4.7★, 28,807 ratings, also $263.86 from 6ave).

3. Best Buy blocked every direct product-page load, so all Best Buy figures come from its search results. That meant I couldn't check who sells this Pro 2 listing. A price of $249.47 against a $274.99 "comparable value" suggests it's a third-party Marketplace seller, not Best Buy.

**Other things to know**
- **Amazon data:** taken from each product page, so the rating counts are exact. All prices are sold by Amazon.com unless note ¹ applies. Every row is for one new unit.
- **Best Buy IDs:** newer listings use 8-digit IDs (12772909, 12734208). These matched the IDs Best Buy's own color picker uses, so I treated them as the SKU.
- **Best Buy counts are reviews:** Best Buy shows a number of reviews, not a separate number of ratings.
- **Colors:** both Max models come in several colors. Amazon prices every Max 2 color at $449. I used Midnight where I could. For the 2024 AirPods Max at Best Buy, Purple was the only color in the results with a rating.
